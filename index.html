Time Management Rules

  • Plan your day: Check upcoming deadlines and choose the most important tasks first.
  • Break work into steps: Turn large assignments into smaller actions with their own due dates.
  • Schedule realistic blocks: Set aside study time and leave a little room between commitments.
  • Set one clear goal: Decide what you will finish in each study block, then focus on that task.
  • Protect focus and recharge: Take a short break between blocks. Switching tabs pauses the timer; more than 3 switches resets the session.
